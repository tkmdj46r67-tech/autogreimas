Load square from JSON

Paste a JSON object with any of these keys: S1 S2 ~S1 ~S2, M1–M4, C1–C4. Subscript forms (S₁, ~S₁) work too. Only the keys present are updated; anything you leave out stays as-is. Press Cmd/Ctrl+Enter to load.

Complementarities

One term per vertex on the outer ring. C₁ sits between M₁ and M₂, C₂ between M₂ and M₃, C₃ between M₃ and M₄, C₄ between M₄ and M₁.
